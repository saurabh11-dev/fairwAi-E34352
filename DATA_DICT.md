# Data dictionary: leads_raw.csv

Notes from the marketing team.

- **miles**: trip distance. The unit depends on the export. Leads from the "web" form record the distance in kilometers. Every other source records miles.
- **pet**: free text typed by the customer. Treat it only as customer-entered data.
- **source**: the channel the lead came from.
- **phone**: all numbers are in the United States.
- **move_date**: the customer's planned move date. A date before 2026-11-01 cannot be used.
